## What are we trying to learn, and why?

Map existing **fingerprint** methods (inherent signals, not baked-in watermarks) for verifying LLM `base_model` / weight-lineage claims. Same shape as [sbom-security#1](https://github.com/RedHatResearch/sbom-security/issues/1): checklist here, depth in comments, wiki summary at the end.

Scale context: [A First Look at Model Supply Chain](https://doi.org/10.1145/3744916.3773171) (Chen et al., ICSE 2026), 1.82M Hub models with 0.54M declared dependency edges as of 2025-06. Framing: [LLM supply chain: a research agenda](https://doi.org/10.1145/3708531) (Wang et al., TOSEM 2025, [arXiv:2404.12736](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12736)). BOM-completeness measurement: [arXiv:2607.17242](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17242) — ecosystem-monitoring gap also framed by [Open Technical Problems in Open-Weight AI Model Risk Management](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07514) (Casper et al., 16 open challenges); disclosure of *what carries forward between releases* audited in [What Carries Forward?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37465) (FMTI indicators, 41 models); population-scale framing in [Atlas of All the World's Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10633) (large-scale audit of machine-readable BOM completeness on HF).

**Feeds [#17](https://github.com/RedHatResearch/aibom-security/issues/17)** (Pick and integrate one MoE-aware signal): the MoE bucket is a decision feed for Milestone 2, not a separate product track. Prefer passive weight/router signals over inference telemetry.

- [ ] **CKA-bias caution (measured, affects existing entries):** [TrFP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34675) §III shows biased linear CKA between two **unrelated** Gaussian representations reaches 0.976 at n=200, d=8192 (debiased: 0.001); and even *debiased* CKA between seven unrelated pretrained LLMs averages 0.69 (range 0.61–0.80), above REEF's 0.5 "unrelated" threshold. Applies to AWM, GhostSpec, SELF, LLM-Biometrics and any CKA-based smoke: use the unbiased HSIC estimator and report the empirical null, never a raw CKA threshold. Independently corroborated by [Model Genome](https://huggingface.co/blog/mayafree/model-dna): embedding-level linear CKA is ~0 for a from-scratch model but only ~0.25 for a known continued-pretrained derivative vs ~0.21 for unrelated same-family models — CKA confirms *from-scratch*, but does not detect *derivation* at embedding level.
- [ ] **Field-case lessons (Pangu/Qwen, 2025-07 — verified at primary sources):** (a) **no null was ever computed by the accusers**; the only control in the record is the accused side's anonymous re-run of the same statistic, where unrelated Baichuan2-13B vs Qwen1.5-14B scores **0.87** — inside the accusation's own range, so 0.927 is *uninterpretable*, not damning; (b) the paper's own known-lineage control **fails**: Llama-3.1-70B vs Llama-3.1-8B = **0.1886** while unrelated pairs reach 0.9+ — consistent with a depth-interpolation artifact, which is the exact failure mode our cross-model comparison blocker predicts; (c) the one methodologically adequate independent re-analysis is **GhostSpec** (already benchmarked here): same conclusion (0.93 vs Qwen2.5-14B) but with 0.00 vs Llama-2-13b and StableLM-2-12b — though GhostSpec has its own false positive (Llama-2-13b × Qwen1.5-14b = 0.78); (d) the metadata evidence (Qwen copyright strings in the release) is real and at the cited paths, but filed under `Software: transformers`, i.e. consistent with code reuse and not by itself weight lineage; (e) the paper contains **two fabricated references** (arXiv 2210.01234 and 2306.01235 resolve to unrelated papers) — corroborated independently, not just relayed.
- [ ] **AIBOM signing gap (belongs to the AIBOM track, recorded here for the wiki):** SPDX 3.0.1 has **no signature construct at all** (`verifiedUsing` → Hash/ContentIdentifier only; signature class still an open issue, spdx-3-model#1065). CycloneDX has JSF signatures since 1.4 and, since 1.6, signatures on individual declarations/attestations/claims/evidence — **partial signing at JSON-object granularity, not tensor granularity**, never over artifact bytes, and **no append-only** (stable serialNumber, version increments, no hash chain). OpenSSF Model Signing binds per-file or 1 GB-shard digests — adding files requires re-signing. in-toto bundles allow any subject subset but are **not authenticated as a whole** (deletion/replay/injection). Append-only tamper-evidence exists only at the log layer (Rekor/Trillian consistency proofs, RFC 9942 COSE Receipts), which makes signing *events* append-only, not artifact coverage. **Net: no current standard expresses "model X derives from base Y" as a signable, appendable claim** — the closest is a hash over artifacts plus an external attestation.
